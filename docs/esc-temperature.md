# ESC temperature observations

During the recent log analysis, one ESC channel reported a much higher temperature than the others. The highest recorded temperature was about 92°C on ESC 1.

The high reading continued through a large part of the flight instead of appearing as one short spike. That is worth checking, but a temperature reported by an ESC does not tell me the exact motor winding or power transistor temperature.

I will first identify the physical ESC corresponding to channel 1, check airflow, mounting, solder joints and mechanical loading, and compare temperatures using an independent measurement if available. The ESC model and temperature-sensor location also need checking.

The ESC electrical telemetry had inconsistent voltage/current scales compared with the power module, so I will not assume that those readings are calibrated.

This is an open issue. I have not confirmed an ESC failure or a final cause.
